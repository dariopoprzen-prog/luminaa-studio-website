About Us

Luminaa Studio is a small team building practical software for content creators.

Luminaa Studio grew out of firsthand experience with how much time daily community work takes — reading messages, not missing important contacts, keeping track when things get busy. Luminaa is built for exactly that: a tool made by someone who knows the work firsthand.

Our goal: build tools that genuinely help without taking away control — every decision stays with the creator.
